## Users

Repository evidence identifies security engineering, platform engineering, AI platform teams, IT and finance operations, product engineering teams, and technical founders as the intended audience. For this redesign, the working assumption is that the home page must first persuade engineering leaders who are evaluating agent systems that can touch production, money, communications, or privileged tools. This priority is inferred and remains open for confirmation.

## Product Purpose

Caimeo is a suite for controlled agentic work. Forseti governs real-world actions, Tyche rehearses and replays agent behavior, Brainstack gives agents shared memory, and Valhalla organizes complex delivery through explicit roles, review, and stop conditions. The public site should make that sequence understandable and turn one concrete workflow or problem into a qualified pilot conversation.

## Positioning

The suite joins durable memory, deterministic policy, reproducible simulation, and structured multi-agent delivery without depending on one model vendor, agent framework, workflow runner, or hardware cycle. Forseti is the commercial entry point, Tyche is the strongest expansion path, and Valhalla remains selective and high-touch.

## Operating Context

Buyers are already piloting agents in workflows that involve deployments, payments, vendor or customer communications, tickets, external APIs, memory, evaluations, and audited delivery. They need to identify one narrow workflow, its blast radius, the real approvers, and the evidence required before autonomy can widen.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Forseti uses typed intents and deterministic `ALLOW`, `DENY`, or `REQUIRES_APPROVAL` decisions before release to a deterministic action plane.
- Tyche defines seeded scenario packs, runs controlled comparisons, and exports replay evidence.
- Brainstack provides shared, inspectable memory across agent tools and machines.
- Valhalla structures clarification, planning, implementation, specialist audit, and bounded convergence.
- The public story is Web2-first, self-hosted, and vendor-agnostic. Do not lead with blockchain, tokens, NFTs, hardware, or generic agent-platform claims.
- Public pricing remains contact sales. Internal pricing targets must not appear on the site.
- Preserve existing routes, live versus coming-soon status, working interactions, and accessibility behavior unless product evidence requires a change.

## Product Principles

- Lead with one concrete risk and one narrow first win.
- Show the mechanism instead of claiming generic intelligence.
- Keep human approval, evidence, and operator control visible.
- Sell the suite as a sequence: control, confidence, memory, and disciplined delivery.
- Preserve deployment and vendor choice.
